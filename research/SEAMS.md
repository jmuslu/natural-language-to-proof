# The six seams: what is enforced, and what still breaks

Decision snapshot: core repairs and the C/D support/export work are committed through `abf7364`, with the graph capability gate in `185691c`; the explicit ProofWriter domain adapter and closed-condition oracle repair are subsequent candidates. The integration checkpoint passed 1,138 Haskell checks across eleven suites, with 19 oracle compile negatives and two positive clients. Python's original 38 solver/runner checks passed; the new domain adapter adds 17 passing tests. The closure repair has its own targeted checks described below; its full integration gate is pending. Test success establishes the stated contracts, not general English understanding.

## 1. Category → type

**Keep the typed compositional interface; do not treat a type check as a meaning check.** `Syntax.Category.catTy` interprets NP as a generalized quantifier, `(e→t)→t`; a name's `e` constant is lifted lexically. Embedded clauses have proposition type `s→t`. `Semantics.Intensional.applyIntensional` and `liftsTo` handle declared intensional parameter lifting, so the base category interpretation alone is not the complete lexical type contract. `Pipeline` now checks and translates each returned candidate through one shared backend; `srReadings` and the selected fields therefore agree.

The Phase B feature repair preserves the matched `[dcl]` feature through an endomorphic modifier. Real `is not` no longer turns its result into featureless S. Restrictive subject relatives attach below the determiner: `Every cat that is red is big` yields `∀x((cat(x) ∧ red(x)) → big(x))`, and the licensed-model regression passes. The 29 semantic-repair checks also cover existential and negated restrictions, feature incompatibility and a shared existential object under coordination.

The same corpus model still returns a wrong, well-typed second reading of `Bob likes and Anne hates the cat`: `likes(Bob) ∧ hate(Anne,cat)`, alongside the correct selected shared-object reading. NP coordination also retains a spurious `cat dog` interpretation. On the 120-theory OWA development prefix, 16 typed DRS occurrences fail the reference converter: eight mix a conditional with a spurious fact and eight contain an incorrect nested conditional. Zero *converted-but-wrong* outputs in that diagnostic is not zero wrong typed meanings. POS backoff improves exact conversion from 86.7% to 93.0% of 2,244 theory sentence occurrences, but its scope is lexical evidence, not a stronger type system.

The restored modifier probe supplies another counterexample to overclaiming: every returned alternative is closed and type `t`, yet only one of three generated modifier fusions is class-licensed. Its subject/object asymmetry is documented in `RESTORED.md`. Do not repair these failures with confidence scores or a claim that all lexical templates are correct.

## 2. DRT truth → claim table

**Keep whole scoped conditions and preserve their binding environment.** `Verify.ClaimKind.assertedClaims` canonicalizes the supplied DRS once and extracts only whole top-level non-presupposed conditions. A negation, implication or disjunction is one condition; its inner atoms are Embedded. `Verify.Reported.splitClaims` separates status without flattening subordinate boxes. The 57 assertion checks test this boundary, and compile negatives reject confirming an extracted embedded claim.

The real `Bob says that the cat is not red` example keeps `red(cat)` under both the report and negation; it does not assert that atom. A matrix factive projects Presupposed content without a duplicate Reported row. Those changes fix concrete claim-table errors rather than merely changing display labels.

The Phase D exporter carries entire DRS universes and conditions into the solver. Its 22 tests cover implication binding, negation, independently freshened discourse existentials, query isolation, residual free references and preservation of attitude/presupposition constructors. Unsupported content does not become an ordinary asserted predicate.

The audit found a concrete second scope leak: `ClaimTable` has `ctUniverse`, but a `Claim`, `Confirmed` token and `Fused` item contain a `CanonCond`, without the enclosing witness environment. In a two-object model with P(a), not P(b), and false q, both `exists x.P(x)` and `exists y.(P(y) implies q)` are true. Truth-computing local oracles use the respective witnesses a and b. Separate canonicalization renames both to r0; before the repair, fusion accepted P(r0) and P(r0) implies q and detached false q. Neither document alone did so. Joint canonicalization and rigid a/b constants preserved the distinction. This was reproduced with hand-authored documents and an explicit finite-model evaluator, not an observed failure of the external receipt oracle (which uses rigid run IDs).

The bounded repair makes `Oracle.covers` and `check` refuse conditions with free canonical references. `freeCanonRefs` tracks the actual binders: an implication's antecedent universe scopes over its consequent, while negation/disjunction binders stay local. Open conditions return Unknown before either custom oracle callback is evaluated; they cannot obtain confirmation tokens. Contextual claims remain in the claim table for inspection. Closed quantified, negative and attitude conditions still pass the scope check. `ScopeOracleSpec` tests these boundaries; `runghc -isrc research/probe-claim-scope.hs` preserves the countermodel and now expects the unsafe admission to be blocked.

This deliberately reduces coverage: the old `A student arrived. She smiles.` whitelist fixture now produces no confirmed bare conditions, because their existential witness is only bound by the outer box. Its test remains with expected abstention; a rigid named positive control still earns reward. A richer future API could carry closed boxes or explicit witness environments through tokens and fusion. The whole-DRS solver already keeps these boxes and remains unchanged by this restriction. Structural top-levelness and standalone logical closure are separate requirements.

## 3. Intensional semantics → epistemic status

**Keep world-sensitive complements and status markers separate from assertion.** `Semantics.Intensional`, `CAtt`, `CPresup`, and `Verify.Reported` provide the boundary: reported content remains under its ordered source chain, and factive projection remains marked as presupposition. A top-level `say(Bob,K)` can be an asserted claim about an utterance without asserting K. The 97 intensional checks include matrix and nested factivity; the nested case retains the enclosing report's source chain exactly once.

The real quote `The cat is red, boasted Bob` exposed a lexical-category escape: the baseline asserted both red(cat) and boasted(cat,Bob). The bounded quotation guard now rejects all nonattribution roots for its recognized suffix pattern; the licensed-model example abstains. Tests cover named and definite speakers, forward and inverted suffixes, and preservation of ordinary comma adjective lists. This is not coverage of arbitrary quotations, adverbial tails or dialogue.

`Bob sees that the cat is red` originally failed to parse. Attested complement-category repair now produces a Reported reading and logs unresolved perceptual versus supervisory sense. Perceptual seeing is factive; supervisory/ensure usage need not be. A global lemma switch would conflate those cases. The logged non-factive default is a known disambiguation limitation, not a theoretical claim that perception is non-factive. Primary sources and the policy are in `FINDINGS.md`.

Neither attribution, presupposition nor a high parser score creates oracle evidence. The guarantee is representation/status preservation under the implemented lexical choices; an incorrect sense or unrecognized quote construction can still produce a well-typed wrong representation.

## 4. Monotonicity → abstention

**Retain proofs from accepted premises, but require completeness for Unknown.** `bench/reasoning/solver.py` has two stated semantics: signed function-free Horn closure and classical first-order Z3. They are not interchangeable: Horn has no classical contraposition, and unrestricted disjunction/existentials exceed its accepted fragment. Both reject unsupported scopes as residuals and abstain on contradictions under their respective checks. Proof support names premise indices, including rule evidence; it is conditional on the parser's assertions and does not create a Haskell `Confirmed` capability.

The solver tests demonstrate that a partial theory can retain a positive or negative proof, while a partial theory cannot certify Unknown. Z3 requires countermodels to both query polarities, a consistent accepted premise set, and no missing/residual content before returning Unknown. The runner's skeptical policy requires agreement across all returned combinations; its credulous policy is an exploratory alternative, not permission to assert a conclusion true under only one reading. A combination cap forces non-top policies to abstain. The CCG beam is separately disclosed, so “all readings” still means all returned meanings, not exhaustive natural-language ambiguity.

Anaphora introduces a second completeness issue: an exported query that resolves to a context existential contains a cross-box reference. The initial solver interface has no shared assignment environment, so that query is residual rather than silently treated as a fresh constant or existential. Export tests pin down this conservative behavior.

### Benchmark sortal conventions are part of the logic contract

The exact constants are `sortalThing = "thing"` and `sortalPerson = "person"` in `src/NL2Proof/Semantics/CCGSem.hs` (with `isSortal` checking exactly those names). The existing `Bench.ProofWriter.drsToStatement` comparison adapter names antecedent-bound references from these predicates and drops bound sortals because the benchmark's rules are unsorted. Its generic `atomsOf` omission can also reach a bound sortal in a consequent; that implementation should not be copied as a global inference rewrite.

The general Python solvers correctly retain sortals. Thus `red(Bob)` and `∀x(person(x) ∧ red(x) → big(x))` do not prove big(Bob) without person(Bob). In a gold-free hand control, both Horn and Z3 returned Unknown; explicitly applying the benchmark domain convention changed both to True. A negative-head control changes Unknown to False. This is formula strengthening under a declared dataset convention, not a meaning-preserving English repair, and it explains why old normalized translation coverage need not predict strict-solver answers.

The candidate `bench/reasoning/domain.py` exposes `normalize_theory(drs, policy='strict')`. Strict is identity. An explicitly selected `proofwriter` policy removes only direct positive unary person/thing guards on variables bound in the antecedent of an eligible top-level flat single-head Horn rule. A guard's own provenance must consist of the corresponding local cues someone/people or something/things. Whole-sentence fallback provenance that merely contains a cue is insufficient. The retained body must be nonempty and range-restrict the head.

It does not erase ground properties, lexical person/thing predicates, negative guards, consequences, existential top boxes, nested rules, disjunctions, reports or presuppositions. Every removed condition, source provenance and original path is returned in decision metadata; top-level proof indices stay stable. The 17 tests include these negative cases and both solvers' signed outcomes. The runner must allow this only for an explicit ProofWriter condition, preserve strict results as a baseline, and record the policy in configuration hashes/results. No gold logical form or label is an adapter input.

At the initial cache audit, 15 complete records were available, all ProverQA; three top-theory bound sortal guards appeared, and the adapter was not applied to them. Once ProofWriter rows arrived, a fixed first-five gold-free probe compared the two policies on the default parser's top meanings. All five theories were partial. The adapter removed all 24 direct bound guards, whose individual provenance leaves were people, things, someone or something. Horn and Z3 agreed: strict abstained on all five; the explicit policy still abstained on four and proved False for `AttNoneg-OWA-D5-283-Q4`. This demonstrates a real representation-policy effect and preservation of partial-theory abstention elsewhere, not an accuracy gain: no labels or gold formulas were read. Reproduce with `research/.venv/bin/python research/probe-domain-policy.py research/runs/phase-d/parses-default.jsonl --limit 5`; the driver ignores incomplete final cache lines and never opens sample files.

## 5. Semiring → fusion

**Use exact source-dependent support, not an uncorrelated strongest class or vote count.** `Provenance.Support` is a finite antichain of sufficient evidence-token sets. `supportTimes` combines all premises in a path, and `supportPlus` combines alternative paths, removing redundant supersets. Evidence classes remain attached to the tokens in each environment. Withdrawing an oracle path can leave supported testimony without leaving its oracle status behind.

`Genealogy` computes the least fixed point from admissible seeds. Unseeded cycles remain unsupported; repeated hearsay is idempotent; independent surviving paths remain available after withdrawal. The integrated Strength suite passes 53 checks, including exhaustive finite algebra cases, source withdrawal versus recomputation, rule dependencies, nested speakers, class correlation, immutable claim binding and prevention of verdict-only oracle seeds. The substrate suite passes 115 checks after retaining the existential-abstention fixture and adding the rigid control. `Fusion.entailedWithSupport` retains proof labels rather than only an unlabeled consequence list. `STRENGTH.md` states the algebra and experimental detail.

This earns integration as a bookkeeping guarantee under trusted graph justifications. It does not verify the validity of an arbitrary caller-supplied inference edge, the honesty of a named source, or a probability of truth. Provenance leaves are conservatively treated as source dependencies; source namespace conventions matter. Finite antichains can grow exponentially: the recorded independent-pair family reaches 1,024 minimal environments at ten pairs. No truncation is hidden, and no corpus-wide latency claim follows from those small measurements. Natural-text parsing and the generated retraction benchmark are separate evidence from the algebraic laws.

## 6. Types → oracle lock

**Retain the sealed structural witness boundary, and state what it does not authenticate.** `Claim` has a nominal role and a hidden constructor. `assertedClaims` is the public route to an asserted witness; arbitrary promotion helpers and bare-CanonCond confirmation are removed. `Fact` retains `factAssertion`, so callers cannot replace its content by updating a bare canonical-condition field. `Oracle.confirm` accepts the asserted witness and emits an immutable token whose claim and oracle identity cannot be changed by record updates.

Six deliberately invalid clients compiled before the repair: raw-condition confirmation, embedded extraction, index coercion, asserted wrapping, candidate promotion and Fact content mutation. They now fail for the intended admission reason. The strengthened gate checks 19 negative clients plus two positive clients and is part of every pushed commit's gates. The integrated graph test additionally shows that a bare Boolean `Verdict.Confirmed` cannot seed strong support. The runtime closure gate adds the independent requirement that an asserted witness's bare condition must bind all of its own references before confirmation.

This is an API guarantee, not source authentication or a proof that an external checker is sound. Callers can author a DRS and supply an Oracle implementation; a structural witness does not prove that the text was parsed correctly or that a custom oracle is trustworthy. A successful solver entailment check also does not upgrade asserted-unconfirmed premises into verified world facts. Keep diagnostic hypothesis checking separate from token-producing confirmation, retain the whole-DRS inference path for scoped reasoning, and assess external receipts/checkers within their declared domains.

## Resulting decisions

The boundary mechanisms that earn their place are scoped DRSs, per-reading shared translation, explicit residual/abstention handling, source-correlated support and typed oracle admission with a closure check. None warrants the claim “typed means true.” The strongest remaining risks are lexical/derivational meaning errors, bounded ambiguity search, limited coverage for contextual claims, and dataset-specific logical conventions. Keep these visible in evaluation and reviewer guidance rather than folding them into one accuracy number or an unqualified safety claim.
