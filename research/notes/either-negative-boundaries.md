The independent direct controls choose unrelated co-satisfiable predicates so
both-true distinguishes inclusive OR from XOR. A source pair whose properties
sound antonymous is not permission to infer exclusivity from vocabulary. Where
explicit not-both occurs, its own licensed composition supplies NOT(AND).
A benchmark solver's opt-in XOR interpretation of an either flag is a separate
execution/semantics contract, never an alteration of literal-English gold or a
reason to pool incompatible acceptance panels. Do not use exactly-one inference
to validate a surface-only marker implementation.

## Fail-closed integration checks to send to the implementation team

1. A pending marker cannot form a root, disappear through punctuation, or take
   an open lexical N/N predicate/identity route. Unsupported determiner/pronoun
   either senses remain outside this construction rather than becoming facts.
2. Pair only the actual direct explicit OR edge, with matching licensed category,
   feature, subject-slot interface and source/token occurrence. AND, comma-only,
   repeated markers, mismatched features and descendant/foreign-source OR cannot
   discharge the pending marker. No synthetic tags to force a positive test.
3. Carry a completed pair through only the reviewed envelope. Full token/span
   coverage is required: no accepting a positive prefix while ignoring explicit
   not-both, or-both, outer NOT, a topic, a conditional or an attitude.
4. Preserve occurrence evidence in packing/selection and lowering. Distinct
   marker attachments with the same Boolean term must not collapse into one
   arbitrary annotation. Ordinary OR has no completed-pair evidence.
5. In the direct controls the marker must reach exactly root OR and nowhere else;
   assert OR only, embed atoms. Same DRS/claims for the unmarked comparator,
   marker=false. No invented XOR, predicate named either, or extra positive atom.
6. Outer negation is not in the first three gold controls. A future NOT(OR)
   control would require OR to stay Embedded and its marker path to descend
   through NOT; do not extrapolate approved direct-root support. Existing seven
   controls remain later scope/negative obligations, with exact gold untouched.
7. On unsupported embedding, no silent evidence loss or root flattening. A
   candidate may abstain, but must not convert an explicit XOR source into the
   simple inclusive prefix or use ambiguous provenance as a claimed exact path.
8. Later runtime acceptance must compare all unchanged190/202 cases and any
   separately frozen direct controls with matching source/binary/case seals;
   report newly wrong, wrong→no and no→exact separately. This note supplies
   independent expected boundaries, not observations or an authorization to run.
