Contract `strict-English-person-v1` uses person-restricted quantifiers:
Everyone P = forall x.(person(x) -> P(x)); Someone P = exists x.(person(x)&P(x)).
Properties are extensional at the evaluation context; no temporal or permanence
claim is licensed. Written adjectives remain unary predicates, not events.
Under Everyone, person is an antecedent restriction, not a root assertion.
Under Someone, person and the property share one root witness. Someone is not
rational means exists x.(person(x)&not rational(x)), not not-exists rational
person and not a universal negative. No existential import for Everyone.

The two source universals preserve all scope: rational OR emotional (or both)
is inclusive in the consequent. Spiritual XOR atheistic is OR plus a negated
conjunction in the consequent. Neither disjunct is independently Asserted; the
whole universal implication is Asserted. Person, OR wrapper/disjuncts, and XOR
negative wrapper/body (where present) are Embedded in preorder. Each disjunction
annotation is at the consequent path; either=false for no surface either, true
for the explicit either case. Metadata is not itself an exclusivity operator.

Same-subject OR/XOR scopes inside the quantifier. Closed negation applies to the
property before existential closure in Someone-not. The intended terms and
binders constrain implementations without assuming current categories/outputs.
