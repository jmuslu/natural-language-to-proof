## Units2/3/4: meanings before observations

For horses, All:NP[nb]/N applies to derived N athletic horses. With independently
intersective athletic under a fixed extensional context, restriction is
horse(x) and athletic(x), head first. Shared-subject copular conjunction yields
fast(x) and strong-willed(x), one individual, no collective/group witness.
DRS `[|[x|horse(x),athletic(x)]⇒[|fast(x),strong-willed(x)]]` asserts only the
whole implication. Embedded traversal is horse,athletic,fast,strong-willed;
Reported/Presupposed/disjunctions empty. Fast's contextual scale is held fixed,
not deleted; strong-willed is one lexical adjective, no arbitrary splitting.

For birds, Every:NP[nb]/N gives lambda R.forall x(bird(x) implies R(x)). Each
transitive VP has category (S[dcl] backslash NP)/NP before its object is applied;
objects are generalized quantifiers over fixed references in this contract.
Shared-subject OR forms lambda x.(know(x,troposphere) or revere(x,Joziah)).
The whole DRS is `[|[x|bird(x)]⇒[|[|know(x,troposphere)] OR
[|revere(x,Joziah)]]]`. Asserted is only implication; Embedded is bird,wholeOR,
know,revere in traversal order. No existential main witness, Reported or
Presupposed conditions. Either=true records the overt marker; explicit or both
requires inclusive OR, not XOR. Fixed definite reference does not claim that
existence/uniqueness accommodation is generally solved.

No first parser divergence has been observed for these new rows. Future Explain
must distinguish actual offered categories, lemma/head order, shared-subject
scope, object identity and claim status. A type-correct OR of two separately
quantified bird clauses is not the intended single binder merely because it
looks similar at sentence level.

## Comparison and coverage limit

Boxer2008's lexical/category distinction (REFERENCES §§3.2–3.3 account) warns
against treating every N/N modifier/compound as intersection; the blocked examples
exercise precisely that limit. Pinned ccg2lambda2015 universal determiner entries
and shared-subject VP coordination provide force/interface comparisons, not
independent proof that these full inputs are parsed faithfully. Its two-slot NP
restriction interface differs from our Q; retain scope and lexical contracts
rather than importing templates wholesale.

The two additions yield another modified-adjectival source from a distinct record,
but do not close the narrow single-VP named-object transitive gap with an
independent record. Further full-source families can be recovered only with their
extra scopes/modifiers intact, or explicitly documented adaptations that are not
counted as unchanged-source evidence. Do not call two clauses extracted from one
source record two independent samples, and do not claim population fidelity from
these compact manually chosen examples.
