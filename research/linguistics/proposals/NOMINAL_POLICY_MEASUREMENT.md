wrong selected answer. Check all retained readings of primary/secondary target
cases against the same gold, including scope, predicate identity and claim
status. Report wrong alternatives even when selected output improves; do not
hide them behind an aggregate exact score or quietly filter readings. These
checks use existing scorer meaning comparison, never a new equivalence rule.

wrong, unparsed, incomplete or errored; any new wrong-but-typed selected output;
any altered unrelated selected meaning without a justified independently reviewed
construction explanation; target wrong alternatives or lost scope/domain/claim
fidelity; mode or artifact integrity mismatch. Extra exact gains cannot compensate
for these failures. Preserve rejected results and Explain first-divergence
hypotheses separately; no immediate template broadening or gold repair follows
from a disappointing result. A missing gain is a diagnostic outcome, not grounds
to shrink denominator or repeat until success.
