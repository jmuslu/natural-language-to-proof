🔍 What is this thing, really?

A project that’s trying to build a careful “truth machine” — and this is an honest look at how far along it is.

The dream: a machine that reads writing, reasons about it super carefully, and only believes something once it’s checked against the real world — aimed at finding trading signals.

How it’s supposed to flow

📖
Read
turn English into logic
⇢
🧠
Reason
chain facts into a claim
⇢
🔬
Check
test it against reality
⇢
💾
Remember
keep only what passed

Each box is built and works on its own — but the arrows aren’t connected yet. It’s parts, not one running machine.

The parts, in plain words

🔒The Lie-Detector Lock
★ Actually works — and genuinely clever

You literally cannot sneak an unchecked “fact” into the reward system — the computer refuses to build. This is the standout piece.

📖The Grammar Reader
Great — but plugged into nothing

Reads real English sentences ~99% correctly (tested on sentences it hadn’t seen). Impressive… and sitting alone on the shelf.

🧩The Reasoning Puzzle-Solver
Only on made-up puzzles

Solves tiny logic puzzles it invented for itself. A dumb brute-force shortcut almost ties it — so it’s a test dummy, not real reasoning yet.

🧠The Brain & Toolbox
Lots of tools, tested one by one

Memory, uncertainty math, “learn the rule,” “know when to shut up.” All neat, all tested separately — never switched on together.

💰The Money Test
Tried for real — didn’t work yet

Every actual market test so far failed or was biased. The trading idea itself hasn’t been proven.

🧰 The honest truth: Right now it’s a workshop full of well-made tools — not a finished machine. The safety lock is real and smart. The reader is strong but lonely. Most of the rest works only on pretend data. And the big promise — turning writing into a money-making signal — hasn’t come true yet.

That’s not failure; it’s an early, honestly-labeled stage: the pipes are being made trustworthy before anything real flows through them.
