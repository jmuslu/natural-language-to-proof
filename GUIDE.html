1 · Compiled proof substrate (Haskell — the trustworthy core)

🔒Verify/Oracle.hs ★type-level

The truth gate. A `Confirmed` value can only be minted by the external oracle (its constructor isn't exported).

proves: Truth enters the reward path through ONE compiler-enforced door; verdict is 3-valued (Confirmed/Refuted/Unknown), never collapsing Unknown→false.

⚠ watch: It guarantees WHERE truth enters, not that the oracle is RIGHT. A wrong oracle still confirms wrong claims.

🧾Verify/Claims.hs—

Atomic claims tied to the derivations that produced them.

proves: Each claim is addressable and linked to its proof.

🔌Verify/External.hsexternal

Where external validation receipts enter the oracle/fusion/reward seam.

proves: Real receipts slot into the same gate as internal ones.

⚠ watch: Only as trustworthy as the receipt producer, which this seam doesn't authenticate.

🌳Genealogy.hs—

The derivation DAG. `grounded` = least fixed point; `retract` = precise withdrawal.

proves: A claim can't ground itself around a cycle (anti-self-agreement); a claim with a 2nd independent derivation survives a retraction.

⚠ watch: Grounding tracks SUPPORT STRUCTURE, not correctness — a confirmed-but-false premise still grounds everything above it.

🏷️Provenance.hs—

Provenance tagging (a tree; every fold-answerable question).

proves: Every derived thing carries where it came from.

🎁Reward.hs—

The RL reward signal — and it REQUIRES a `Confirmed` argument.

proves: Reward literally cannot be computed from unverified claims.

⚠ watch: Vector-valued; reward correctness still depends on the oracle being right.

🔗Semantics/Lambda.hstype-level

Montague simply-typed lambda calculus over types e and t; `typeOf` type-checks a derivation.

proves: A derivation's meaning provably inhabits its declared type.

⚠ watch: Type-correct ≠ meaning-correct. Faithful translation of the sentence is a separate, unmeasured target.

📚Syntax/Category.hsCCGbank

Maps a CCG category to the semantic type its meaning must have (`catTy`, a functor).

proves: The syntax→semantics correspondence is mechanical and checkable per lexical entry.

⚠ watch: `catTy PP = tyGQ` is a STIPULATED choice; features are erased. A wrong stipulation is silent.

📖Syntax/CCG*.hs (parser family) ★CCGbank (licensed, uncommitted)

CKY parser, supertagger, CCGbank ingest, type-change induction — reads real English into derivations.

proves: 99.9% lexicon type-coverage; 99.1% held-out parse on a DISJOINT split given gold supertags.

⚠ watch: Measures SYNTAX generalization within 1990s WSJ register only; numbers are 'Historical' (not re-run in CI); says nothing about meaning. ISLAND: nothing downstream consumes its output.

💬DRT/*.hs—

Turns a CCG derivation into a Discourse Representation Structure (provenance-tagged).

proves: Meaning representations are built and threaded across a discourse.

⚠ watch: Built but the Python reasoning pipeline NEVER consumes a DRS. The DRS→typed-fact extractor doesn't exist. This is the gap between parsing and reasoning.

🎭Stage2/*.hs (RSA, SDRT, Model, …)—

Pragmatics: generate alternative readings, rank them (Rational Speech Act), enumerate finite models.

proves: A probabilistic distribution over readings can be produced.

⚠ watch: Exploratory; ranks readings but isn't wired to the oracle or the reasoning engine.

🩹Fusion / Pipeline / Collapse / Trace / Fragment—

Substrate plumbing: fuse confirmed claims, run text→DRS, pick a reading, trace, fixed eval set.

proves: The stages connect into one pipeline.

2 · Epistemic graph + fact brain (Python)

🕸️epistemic_graph.pycorpus

Bidirectional trust graph over claims/sources/refs/outcomes; the Python analog of Genealogy.

proves: Trust originates ONLY from external checks; a pure corroboration cluster (echo chamber) stays at 0.

⚠ watch: `propagate` is inert-by-default & the update SHAPE is a deliberately-open question; 'trust' here is an exploratory score, not a probability.

📊build_graph.pycorpus

Assembles exploratory graph scores from corpus records + reference proposals.

proves: Scores keep their source provenance.

⚠ watch: These scores are PROPOSED/owner-judged, not checked factual labels — don't read them as verdicts.

🎚️fit_graph.pycorpus

Fits the graph's trust-update PARAMETERS against held-out externally-checked outcomes.

proves: Params only help when real external evidence exists; an echo chamber can't be fit into a signal (train/test).

⚠ watch: Tunes params within ONE fixed topology — it is NOT searching for the right mechanism shape (that would be the trap).

🧠brain.py / brain_query.pycorpus

libSQL vector store over the graph: semantic retrieval, synthetic inference, directed exploration.

proves: Facts get organized by MEANING so you can retrieve and propose what to check next.

⚠ watch: Embeddings are geometry only and never create trust; retrieval coverage is descriptive — similar neighbours do NOT answer a question.

🔡brain_embed.py—

Turns claim text into a unit vector (model2vec, or offline hashing).

proves: Embedding provenance is recorded; geometry stays separate from trust.

⚠ watch: The hashing backend captures LEXICAL, not deep semantic, similarity — labelled so, but easy to over-read.

🗺️brain_map.pycorpus

Renders the real brain.db as an interactive 2D semantic-trust map.

proves: A faithful picture: position=meaning, colour=external trust, ring=frontier.

⚠ watch: A picture of the store, not new evidence.

🧭brain_exploration.py / brain_agenda.pycorpus

Nominates mechanism chains from uncertain premises; turns exploration into a ranked research agenda.

proves: The system proposes its own next external checks.

⚠ watch: Semantic adjacency supplies NEITHER a causal edge NOR a checked conclusion; every agenda item is `proposed`, never decided.

🧪brain_build.py / brain_evidence.py / brain_test.pycorpus

Build the brain; the observed-receipt evidence channel; the offline test suite.

proves: Trust is copied verbatim from the graph (tests assert the brain never invents it).

⚠ watch: The evidence channel checks snapshot consistency, not cryptographic producer authenticity.

3 · Corpus, records & temporal data (the REAL data — and it's tiny)

🗃️build_records.pycorpus: 5 records

Builds the curated corpus (schema v2): normalizes, decomposes per-claim, tracks confounds.

proves: The agent STRUCTURES data; every consistency verdict is left null for an external check.

⚠ watch: Five hand-picked records is too small/confounded to be a readiness check for anything.

✅verify_claims.pycorpus

Agent-proposed, reference-backed per-claim verdicts (a proposer, during bootstrap only).

proves: Each verdict cites what it was checked against; 'unresolved' is used honestly and often.

⚠ watch: A per-claim verdict can differ from the owner's GLOBAL label — don't conflate the statement with the endeavour.

🏷️add_evidence_basis.pycorpus

Tags each claim public_reference vs self_reported vs unquantifiable (as data, not a heuristic).

proves: Only public_reference claims belong on the verify frontier.

⚠ watch: A mis-tag silently routes a claim to the wrong kind of check.

🎲confound_check.pycorpus

Permutation test: does format/length ALONE separate the labels better than chance?

proves: If a confound separates the buckets, any content-based result on this corpus is untrustworthy.

⚠ watch: Right now there aren't enough balanced records to conclude anything either way.

🆔export_claims_index.py / propose_claim_refs.pycorpus

Stable claim IDs; deterministic retrieval of the claims a hypothesis leans on.

proves: claim_refs come from retrieval, not an LLM guess.

⚠ watch: claim_refs are ATTRIBUTION, never a proof — a strategy failing doesn't refute the claims it cited.

📅events/build_events.py + add_disclosure_timing.pyevents

Dated event store: bitemporal stamps, no-lookahead; disclosure timing set from disclosure evidence.

proves: The event study can place its window correctly (pre/intraday=same session, after-close=next).

⚠ watch: Timing is set from the DISCLOSURE (not the price reaction — that'd be circular); some timings come from norms, not verified timestamps.

4 · Reasoning engine (a SYNTHETIC Horn-proof checker — a control, not alpha)

⚙️reasoning/engine.pysynthetic worlds

Bounded Horn-clause proof checking. No solver, no access to the reference answer.

proves: A proof is either valid under the task's rules or Unknown (abstention).

⚠ watch: The rules are ASSUMPTIONS of a generated puzzle, NOT facts attested about markets; Unknown ≠ 'no proof exists'.

🧩reasoning/scaffold.pysynthetic worlds

A model picks rule-instances; `deterministic_search` is an exhaustive symbolic BASELINE.

proves: Separates 'model chose well' from 'a dumb solver would too'.

⚠ watch: The dumb baseline nearly ties the model (26/30 vs 30/30) — so 30/30 is NOT evidence of reasoning ability.

📒reasoning/ledger.pyglue

Binds proof leaves to the external run-receipt journal.

proves: Establishes only the consequences of passes_validation(run_id).

⚠ watch: Never establishes the truth of source prose or a strategy's fitness; never reads a model-written receipt field.

🏁reasoning/benchmark.py / run.py / scaffold_run.pysynthetic worlds

Generated-world multi-hop benchmark + harness; an independent reference grades it.

proves: Gold labels are read ONLY for scoring, never sent to the proposer or used as repair.

⚠ watch: These are controlled tasks — NOT market discovery, language extraction, or frontier parity.

5 · Reasoning & uncertainty mechanics (each tested ALONE, on synthetic data)

📐estimand.pysynthetic

Typed quantities (target/units/basis/uncertainty) + conditional numerical updates.

proves: Unknown targets can't be pooled; null claims need positive equivalence evidence.

⚠ watch: Provenance/SNR/coherence do NOT authenticate an observation, establish independence, or calibrate confidence — the external receipt governs belief.

🪜multihop.pysynthetic

Candidate reasoning templates + conditional numerical propagation across hops.

proves: A template can be instantiated and its legs' coherence checked locally.

⚠ watch: Leg coherence is a LOCAL diagnostic, not external verification; writes no trust; qualitative scores never become numerical effects.

🔤reason.pysynthetic

Structural type composition (e/t/→, mirroring the Haskell types).

proves: Matching types = a well-formed application.

⚠ watch: Well-formed ≠ entailment or truth; confidence is METADATA, and multiplying confidences ignores dependence.

↩️retract.pysynthetic

Least-fixed-point grounding + precise retraction (Python mirror of Genealogy).

proves: A claim with independent support survives; the naive 'everything downstream' over-retracts.

⚠ watch: Grounding = support structure, not correctness (same caveat as Genealogy).

🧫grok.py / robust_concept.pysynthetic

Order-invariant induction of a restricted Boolean / signed-conjunction concept.

proves: Recovers a required/forbidden feature set from labeled examples, with held-out selection.

⚠ watch: Describes a HYPOTHESIS, not causal necessity; low training error ≠ truth; contradictory or out-of-class labels leave the fit inconsistent.

🔭discover.pysynthetic

Template-learning + measurement demo built on grok's constructed law.

proves: Tests generalization WITHIN the generator.

⚠ watch: Validates no market mechanism or neuromorphic analogy; coherent checks never manufacture a Sharpe or portfolio number.

🌊surprise.pysynthetic

Causal-filtering updates + retrospective HMM diagnostics (KL of filter vs smoother).

proves: Localizes where a forward pass was later revised.

⚠ watch: `retrospective_surprise` needs FUTURE data — lookahead if misused as live signal; KL/expected-info-gain/free-energy are DIFFERENT quantities; establishes no profitable signal.

📈regime_estimand.pysynthetic

Scalar Kalman state tracking with adaptive-jump and outlier heuristics.

proves: Tracks a drifting/jumping value; adaptive inflation reacts fast to breaks.

⚠ watch: Adaptive inflation AMPLIFIES isolated bad observations; robust inflation DELAYS real jumps; the covariances are diagnostics, NOT calibrated uncertainty.

⏱️online_residual.py / evaluate_online_residual.pysynthetic

One-step forecasts with delayed outcomes; frozen pre-outcome blend weights.

proves: A forecast at t is scored against the target revealed at t+1 before training on it.

⚠ watch: An explicit RECONSTRUCTION of a missing component, not the historical learner; no belief writes, no market claim.

🎛️metalearn.py / selector.pysynthetic

Learn / heuristically choose when to escalate a cheap method to a costly one.

proves: Escalation is judged by paired EXTERNAL losses, latency and cost.

⚠ watch: Method DISAGREEMENT is a diagnostic, not an error label (the paid method can be worse or share the bias); no regret guarantee in shifting contexts.

🌿alternates.pysynthetic

Builds several machineries per mechanic and compares them fairly.

proves: Records WHERE each wins; keeps promising corners rather than crowning one.

⚠ watch: A comparison, not a verdict — none of these is certified as the right default.

🎯acquisition.py / acquisition_evaluation.pysynthetic

Finite Bayesian experiment design under EXPLICITLY SUPPLIED likelihoods.

proves: Which experiment maximizes information / decision value under stated utilities.

⚠ watch: Authenticates no mechanism or observation; NOT financial data; the unconditioned payoff can be zero-mean even when a conditional edge exists.

📋mechanism_spec.pysynthetic

Checks a declared mechanism design for completeness + inventory compatibility.

proves: Design assumptions are explicit and separable from data availability.

⚠ watch: Validates DESIGN completeness only — identifies no causal effect, authorizes no run, certifies no inventory.

📉uncertainty_experiments.py / adaptive_evaluation.pysynthetic

Offline uncertainty/pooling matrix; seeded concept-induction/abstention/escalation experiments.

proves: Reproducible results against independently generated known targets; shifted tests retain failures.

⚠ watch: 'Coverage' = repeated-sim coverage of THAT target, not a 95%-everywhere guarantee; worlds are generated independently of the policy.

🛑abstain.pysynthetic

Calibrates a selective-prediction threshold from independently-checked outcomes.

proves: A finite-family threshold with a risk bound; no passing threshold ⇒ a valid all-abstain.

⚠ watch: The bound assumes INDEPENDENT same-distribution examples — it does NOT certify risk after a quality or context shift.

🧵research_threads.pysynthetic

Executable research counterexamples (exact algebra, finite enumeration, seeded Monte Carlo).

proves: Labels each probe's kind; reusable in future benchmarks.

⚠ watch: Changes no corpus beliefs; see RESEARCH_THREADS.md for the actual hypotheses and limits.

6 · Bridges, pipeline & outcomes (the glue — currently thin / dead-ends)

Ingests checked run verdicts (with replay + revision) into the graph.

proves: The proposition is passes_validation(run_id); revisions retract precisely.

⚠ watch: claim_refs are attribution — never promoted to proofs nor refuted by a strategy's failure; a descriptive grounding leg can't override the independent certificate.

🌉oracle_bridge.pyglue

🔁reverse_propagation_demo.pysynthetic

proves: Shows what the graph does ONCE the attribution link exists.

⚠ watch: The attribution link + outcome are SYNTHETIC; real attribution-logging (which claims a strategy used) is the UN-BUILT prerequisite.

🚰pipeline.pyglue

The orchestrator: receipt journal → Haskell oracle/reward → graph → brain.

proves: Runs the stages and reports which were skipped / what evidence was observed.

⚠ watch: CCGbank ingest checks STRUCTURAL typing only; invoking components ≠ efficacy; today the ingest stage DEAD-ENDS in a printout, not into the graph.

❌fixture_futures_reversal.pyrefuted
